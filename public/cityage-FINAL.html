The City

Where Asia meets the Americas.
And what gets built next.

Vancouver is one of the most consequential cities on Earth for a simple reason: it sits at the intersection of the world's two largest economies, on the edge of the fastest-growing ocean, in a country making enormous bets on energy, technology, and infrastructure.

No other North American city is more directly shaped by the decisions being made in Beijing, Tokyo, Seoul, and Singapore. No other city on the continent is more exposed — and more positioned — for what comes next in the Pacific Century.

The Next Vancouver brings together the leaders building that future: the investors, the ministers, the mayors, the engineers, and the founders who are deciding, right now, what Vancouver becomes.

The Format

One day.
No panels. No pitches.

The Next Vancouver is an invitation-only gathering of 100 leaders operating under Chatham House Rules. No press. No broadcasting. No keynote speakers selling their latest book.

What you get instead: a day of real conversations between people who are actually building things — and the connections that follow from that.

Invite-Only 100 Leaders Chatham House Rules One Day No Press
Who Attends

The people
building what comes next.

Ministers and senior government officials. Fund managers and institutional investors. Founders and technology leaders. Urban planners, architects, and infrastructure builders. The mayors and city-builders shaping how Vancouver grows.

Access is not purchased. It is earned through the referral chain and the verification process that has kept this network trusted for 14 years.

The First Next Vancouver

It started
here.

The inaugural Next Vancouver brought together 100 leaders for a day of conversations about the city's future — its economy, its housing crisis, its role in the Pacific, and the decisions that would define the next decade.

The conversations that happened in that room are still happening. That is what CityAge does.

Our Founding Thesis

The Urban
Planet.

We live on the urban planet. Two percent of Earth. Everything happens here.
"We live on the urban planet. Two percent of Earth. Everything happens here."
M
Miro Cernetig CEO & Publisher — CityAge

The Urban Planet is the two per cent of the Earth where people mostly are — and where innovation and investment happens. Sometimes it's a vast urban region. Sometimes it's a small and distant place. All of it is connected — through modern technology and human endeavour — into a single web of decisions.

Iqaluit is as connected to the Urban Planet as New York, Beijing or Brussels.

CityAge was founded on a single observation: the decision-makers responsible for building the next century rarely get to talk to each other. Not the right ones. Not under conditions where trust gets built and decisions get made.

Fifteen years and 100+ forums later, we've built the network that changes that — 25,000 verified leaders across government, capital, and industry, convened around the problems worth solving.

Two percent of Earth. Everything happens here. We work there.
