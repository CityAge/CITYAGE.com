The Roots

A lesson learned in the field.

Miro Cernetig spent his career as a foreign correspondent and bureau chief for The Globe and Mail — in Beijing, New York, Alberta, the Arctic, and Vancouver. Those postings — from China's rise to Canada's energy heartland to the High North — taught a lasting lesson: narrative that serves the public interest is the most powerful force in moving governments, markets, and minds.

That conviction, recognized with Canada's Michener Award for public-service journalism, is the foundation of this practice.

The Practice

Not noise. Signal.

CityAge builds bespoke, deeply researched campaigns for leaders and organizations whose work deserves to be understood. Each engagement begins with original research and ends with a narrative that can stand up in a newsroom, a boardroom, or a legislature.

The Network

The best minds, at the table.

Three decades in journalism, and fifteen years convening CityAge's network of 25,000 leaders across government, capital, and industry, mean one more thing: for any consequential project, we know how to find the best minds and bring them to the table.

A Record of Consequence
