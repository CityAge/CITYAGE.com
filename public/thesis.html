The Urban Planet Thesis

The 3% that decides everything.

"Cities are 3% of the Earth's surface and more than 80% of its GDP. Every structural challenge of our era — climate, capital, sovereignty — gets solved, or doesn't get solved, in cities."
The Founding Observation

The right people rarely talk to each other.

CityAge was founded on a single observation: the decision-makers responsible for building the next century of urban infrastructure — the mayors, the fund managers, the engineers, the ministers — rarely get to talk to each other. Not the right ones. Not under conditions where trust gets built and deals get done.

The forums, the panels, the conferences — most of them are designed for broadcast, not conversation. The right people are in the room, but they're talking at each other, not with each other. The deals that matter happen in the hallway, or they don't happen at all.

The Thesis

Intelligence for the Urban Planet.

The Urban Planet is not a metaphor. It is a geographic fact. Roughly 3% of the Earth's surface — the cities, the corridors, the capital hubs — is where 80% of global GDP is produced and consumed. It is where climate policy gets funded or defunded. Where Arctic sovereignty is decided. Where the next generation of infrastructure is financed, designed, and built.

The decision-makers who shape that 3% are not short of information. They are short of the right conversations, the right relationships, and the intelligence that tells them where to look next. That is what CityAge provides.

Fourteen Years On

100+ forums. 50+ cities. One network.

Since 2012, CityAge has convened more than 100 invite-only forums across 50+ cities — from Vancouver to Ottawa, from Washington to Helsinki. The network that has emerged from those rooms now numbers 25,000 verified leaders across government, capital, and industry.

The thesis has not changed. The scale has. What began as a single editor's conviction — that the right people in the right room changes everything — is now a platform, a methodology, and a network that operates at the speed of decisions.
