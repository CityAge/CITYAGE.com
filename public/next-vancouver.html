The Question

Where can this region actually win?

Metro Vancouver has an AI advantage — but only in the sectors where this region already leads the world. The A.I. Edition convenes the leaders of those sectors to answer one question: where can Vancouver, British Columbia and Canada actually win with artificial intelligence?

Not AI in the abstract. AI in the industries built on this coast — and the decisions, investments and policies that will determine whether this region leads the AI economy or watches it.

The Sectors

Seven places we lead.

01 Oceans The Pacific gateway meets autonomous shipping, smart ports and marine intelligence.
02 Mining BC's critical minerals and exploration technology feed the machine age.
03 Genomics Vancouver's life-sciences corridor turns health data into the next great export.
04 Energy The grid must be rebuilt for a power-hungry decade.
05 Data Centres The new industrial infrastructure deciding which regions matter.
06 Wildfires Prediction, detection and the fight itself — AI on the front line of BC's defining emergency.
07 Innovation How our universities, companies and governments turn research into companies that stay and scale here.
The Room

One day. One room.

The people deciding whether this region leads the AI economy — mayors, ministers, investors, scientists and the executives building these seven sectors — convened in Vancouver in the first week of November.

The Next Metro Vancouver is in its second year, in partnership with The Vancouver Sun. The first edition, held October 2025, brought together leaders from the Senate of Canada, YVR, Providence Health Care, Genome BC, FortisBC and dozens more.
