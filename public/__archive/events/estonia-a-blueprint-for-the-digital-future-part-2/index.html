Estonia: A blueprint for the Digital Future (Part 2)

A CityAge Select Event

Vancouver, June 17, 2025

space is limited - request your invite

“Its government is virtual, borderless, blockchained, and secure. Has (Estonia) this tiny post-Soviet nation found the way of the future?”
– the New Yorker

The Hon. Liisa-Ly Pakosta
Minister of Justice and Digital Affairs, Estonia

Few countries have embraced the digital world as swiftly as Estonia, a small country that has a globally significant digital footprint.

We invite you to meet The Hon. Liisa-Ly Pakosta, Estonia’s Minister of Justice and Digital Affairs, and her business delegation, to explore the path to the digital future.

Join us November 1st, from 8:30am to Noon in downtown Vancouver. Space is limited, we encourage you to request an invitation in the form below as soon as possible.

More details will be announced soon.
